Upload grades

Upload an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file. The first row must be headers. Required columns: Student ID and either Name or LAST NAME / FIRST NAME / MIDDLE NAME. Every other column (Prelim, Midterm, Semi-Finals, Finals, Final Rating, Remarks, etc.) is shown to the student. Each upload replaces all grades, so always upload your complete sheet. Grades are encrypted, then a new index.html is downloaded for you to upload to your website host.
